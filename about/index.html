About Tempe Commercial Cleaning

22 years serving offices, facilities and commercial buildings across Tempe and the East Valley.

What We Do

Tempe Commercial Cleaning provides recurring commercial cleaning services to offices, multi-tenant buildings and business facilities in Tempe and within a 25-mile radius. We have operated in this market for 22 years.

The service lines we offer—nightly cleaning, floor care and restroom programs—represent work we have done consistently throughout that period. We do not offer every type of cleaning service; we offer the services we can deliver consistently to commercial clients.

How We Work

Every engagement begins with a walkthrough of your facility. The walkthrough produces a written scope of work that specifies what is cleaned, how often, by what method, and who is responsible for what. That scope is the reference point for the entire relationship—not a verbal agreement, not a standard "office cleaning" package filled in with your address.

We assign a consistent crew to each account. The same crew handles your facility on a nightly basis so that the people cleaning your building know where things are, what the priority areas are, and what the access restrictions are. Rotating random crews through an account is one of the most common reasons commercial cleaning relationships fail. We do not operate that way.

Serving Tempe and the East Valley

Tempe is a commercial market with unusual density for its size. The area around Tempe Town Lake, the Mill Avenue entertainment corridor, the university district and the Price Road tech corridor all represent distinct cleaning environments. We have worked in all of them. That familiarity with the market is something we carry into every scope conversation.

We serve businesses within 25 miles of Tempe, including Phoenix, Mesa, Chandler, Scottsdale, Guadalupe, Gilbert, Ahwatukee and Paradise Valley. Drive time from our operations base is something we confirm before committing to any nightly schedule.
