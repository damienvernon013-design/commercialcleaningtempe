Commercial Cleaning Pricing in Tempe, AZ

What drives commercial cleaning costs in the East Valley market, and what to expect when you request a written quote.

How Commercial Cleaning Is Priced in Tempe

Commercial cleaning in the Tempe market is priced on a combination of square footage, frequency, space type and access window. Frequency is the single largest variable: a 4,000 sq ft office cleaned five nights per week costs substantially more per month than the same office cleaned once per week, even though the square footage is identical.

We provide written quotes after a walkthrough rather than publishing a fixed rate card, because two facilities with the same square footage can require different crew hours based on layout, floor type, restroom count and access restrictions. A quote without a walkthrough is an estimate; a quote after a walkthrough is a proposal we stand behind.

Variables That Move the Number

VariableEffect on price
Cleaning frequencyPrimary driver. 5 nights/week vs. once/week can triple the monthly cost for identical square footage.
Square footageLinear relationship above a minimum. Sub-1,000 sq ft spaces carry a minimum charge regardless of area.
Restroom countRestrooms are labour-intensive relative to their square footage. Each multi-fixture restroom adds material time.
Floor typeVCT maintenance, polished concrete programs and carpet extraction are priced separately from general cleaning.
Access windowOvernight access is standard. Daytime or specific-window access requiring crew coordination costs more.
Supply inclusionRestroom supplies (paper, soap, liners) can be included in the program price or billed at cost. We confirm this at quote time.

Requesting a Quote

To receive a written quote for your Tempe facility, submit a request with your facility type, square footage, restroom count and preferred frequency. We respond by the next business day and schedule a walkthrough if the scope warrants one.

We do not publish fixed rate cards because a rate card without a walkthrough sets the wrong expectations. The written proposal after the walkthrough is specific to your building and your schedule, and it is what we price our work against.

Floor Care Pricing

Strip-and-wax programs, scrub-and-recoat services and carpet extraction are priced per visit on a project or scheduled basis, separate from the nightly cleaning contract. Pricing depends on square footage, coat count (for VCT), and access window requirements. Contact us for a floor-specific assessment.
