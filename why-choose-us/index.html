Why Tempe Buildings Choose Us

Specific reasons, not marketing claims. If you manage a commercial facility in the East Valley, here is what distinguishes one cleaning contractor from another.

22 Years in the East Valley Market

We have cleaned commercial buildings in the Tempe area since 2003. That is not a founding story; it is a track record of contract renewals. Cleaning companies that do not perform do not last 22 years in a market where facilities managers talk to each other. Our longest-running accounts have been with us for over a decade.

Written Scope Before Any Work Begins

The most common source of disputes in commercial cleaning is the gap between what a client expected and what a contractor delivered. That gap almost always traces back to an undocumented scope—a verbal agreement, a templated proposal, or a vague "office cleaning" line item.

We produce a written scope of work after every facility walkthrough. The scope specifies exactly what is cleaned, at what frequency, by what method. It is the reference point for everything that follows. If the service does not match the scope, the scope is what we resolve against.

Consistent Crew Assignment

Rotating anonymous crews through an account is one of the most common ways commercial cleaning quality degrades after the first 30 days. When the person cleaning your building changes every week, there is no institutional knowledge of your priorities, your restricted areas, or your access protocols.

We assign a consistent crew to each account. The same people handle your facility on a recurring basis. That is not a feature; it is the baseline standard for a commercial cleaning relationship that does not require constant supervision.

Insured and Bonded

We carry general liability insurance and workers' compensation. A certificate of insurance is included with every written quote. If your building management or property insurance requires documentation, we provide it without delay. Full details are on the insured and bonded page.

Price Transparency

We explain what drives the price of a cleaning program before you sign anything. Frequency, square footage, floor type, restroom count and access window are the five variables that move the number most. We explain each one on the quote and on our pricing page.

Next-Business-Day Response

We do not have a call centre. Quote requests receive a written response by the next business day. Service concerns during an active contract receive a response within one business day from the account manager responsible for your facility.
