What Nightly Commercial Cleaning Covers

Nightly commercial cleaning is a recurring service, not a one-time event. The scope is written before the first crew visit and reviewed at 30 days. Standard nightly scope for a commercial office floor typically includes:

  • Common area vacuuming and hard-floor damp mopping
  • Trash and recycling removal, liner replacement
  • Kitchen and breakroom surface wipe-down and appliance exteriors
  • Restroom disinfection, fixture cleaning and supply replenishment
  • Conference room reset: chairs, surfaces, glass boards
  • Reception and lobby detail
  • Glass entry and interior partition wipe-down

What is not in that list is equally important. We confirm what we do not cover in writing—suite interiors with access restrictions, server rooms, specific equipment surfaces—before you approve the scope. No ambiguity about whose job it is.

Who This Service Suits

Nightly commercial cleaning works best for businesses that have consistent overnight access windows, predictable square footage and a stable occupancy schedule. Office buildings with multiple tenants, professional services firms, financial offices, tech companies on the Price Road corridor, and light-industrial businesses with adjoining offices are the most common clients in the Tempe market.

If your occupancy or access varies week to week, we can discuss a flexible schedule at quote time. The nightly model does not suit every facility, and we say so rather than forcing a bad fit.

Frequency drives price more than square footage does in most cases. A 4,000 sq ft office cleaned 5 nights per week costs more than a 6,000 sq ft warehouse cleaned once a week. We explain the arithmetic on every quote.

How the First 30 Days Work

The walkthrough produces a written scope. The scope is reviewed and signed. The first crew visit is scheduled for a low-occupancy window. After the first two weeks, we schedule a 30-minute in-person review with your facilities contact to confirm the scope is matching expectations. Adjustments at that stage cost nothing. Adjustments after 90 days are renegotiated as scope changes.
