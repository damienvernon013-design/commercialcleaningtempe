How Scope Works

We do not use a standard "office cleaning" package and fill it in with your address. Every scope starts with a walkthrough, a fixture count, a square footage measure and a conversation about access windows and priority areas.

The result is a written scope of work that specifies what gets cleaned, how often, by what method, and by which crew. That document travels with every quote and every contract. If the service does not match the scope, the scope is the reference point for resolution—not a verbal agreement from the sales call.
