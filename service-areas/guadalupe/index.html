About Guadalupe as a Commercial Cleaning Market

  • Guadalupe is an incorporated town of approximately 6,000 residents bordered entirely by Tempe and Phoenix, with commercial activity concentrated primarily along Avenida del Yaqui and along the Baseline Road commercial corridor adjacent to the Ahwatukee border.
  • The town's small geographic footprint (roughly one square mile) means most commercial facilities are small-format offices and retail spaces requiring weekly or bi-weekly programs rather than nightly contracts, with pricing structured accordingly.
  • Guadalupe's commercial buildings along Baseline Road near the I-10 interchange service a mix of light-industrial and professional tenants, and floor types in these spaces are predominantly polished concrete or sealed commercial tile rather than carpeted office environments.
