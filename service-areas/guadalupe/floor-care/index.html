Floor Care for Guadalupe Commercial Buildings

Hard-surface floor maintenance for Guadalupe facilities is handled from our Tempe operations base, approximately 4 miles, 8–12 minutes from our Tempe operations base. Floor care programs in Guadalupe are assessed and scheduled the same way as any other market: a walkthrough that identifies the floor type, the current condition and the access window available for scheduled program visits.

Example: A 5,200 sq ft light-industrial storage and distribution unit along Baseline Road

The floor is bare concrete with no sealer applied. The program covers quarterly autoscrubbing to remove dust and debris accumulation, with a sealer application assessment after the first year to determine whether a penetrating sealer is warranted based on the porosity the autoscrub reveals.

Floor Types We Service in Guadalupe

  • VCT (vinyl composite tile) — strip, refinish, scrub-and-recoat
  • Polished concrete — low-abrasion burnishing, pH-appropriate maintenance
  • Sealed concrete — autoscrub, periodic resealing assessment
  • Commercial carpet tile — extraction, interim encapsulation, spot treatment
  • Specialty surfaces (travertine, terrazzo, hardwood) — assessed individually at walkthrough

Scheduling Around Guadalupe Facilities

Floor care programs in Guadalupe are scheduled for low-occupancy windows, typically weekend nights or holiday periods. We confirm the access window in writing before scheduling and do not reschedule unilaterally.
