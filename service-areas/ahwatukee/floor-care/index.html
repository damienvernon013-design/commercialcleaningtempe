Floor Care for Ahwatukee Commercial Buildings

Hard-surface floor maintenance for Ahwatukee facilities is handled from our Tempe operations base, approximately 14 miles, 18–24 minutes from our Tempe operations base. Floor care programs in Ahwatukee are assessed and scheduled the same way as any other market: a walkthrough that identifies the floor type, the current condition and the access window available for scheduled program visits.

Example: A 4,500 sq ft medical practice on Ray Road

The floor is commercial VCT in the clinical areas and polished concrete in the reception and waiting area. The maintenance program splits into two protocols: a strip-and-recoat cycle for the VCT in clinical corridors (twice annually), and a low-abrasion burnish on the polished concrete (monthly). The two protocols must not be applied to the wrong surface.

Floor Types We Service in Ahwatukee

  • VCT (vinyl composite tile) — strip, refinish, scrub-and-recoat
  • Polished concrete — low-abrasion burnishing, pH-appropriate maintenance
  • Sealed concrete — autoscrub, periodic resealing assessment
  • Commercial carpet tile — extraction, interim encapsulation, spot treatment
  • Specialty surfaces (travertine, terrazzo, hardwood) — assessed individually at walkthrough

Scheduling Around Ahwatukee Facilities

Floor care programs in Ahwatukee are scheduled for low-occupancy windows, typically weekend nights or holiday periods. We confirm the access window in writing before scheduling and do not reschedule unilaterally.
