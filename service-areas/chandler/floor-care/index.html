Floor Care for Chandler Commercial Buildings

Hard-surface floor maintenance for Chandler facilities is handled from our Tempe operations base, approximately 12 miles, 18–22 minutes from our Tempe operations base. Floor care programs in Chandler are assessed and scheduled the same way as any other market: a walkthrough that identifies the floor type, the current condition and the access window available for scheduled program visits.

Example: A 6,500 sq ft downtown Chandler retail-to-office conversion in a historic building

The original terrazzo and sealed concrete floors require stripping of any wax residue from prior maintenance and a resealing with penetrating sealer rather than a topcoat finish. A topcoat on unsealed historic terrazzo traps moisture and damages the surface; this building needs a different protocol than a standard VCT strip-and-wax.

Floor Types We Service in Chandler

  • VCT (vinyl composite tile) — strip, refinish, scrub-and-recoat
  • Polished concrete — low-abrasion burnishing, pH-appropriate maintenance
  • Sealed concrete — autoscrub, periodic resealing assessment
  • Commercial carpet tile — extraction, interim encapsulation, spot treatment
  • Specialty surfaces (travertine, terrazzo, hardwood) — assessed individually at walkthrough

Scheduling Around Chandler Facilities

Floor care programs in Chandler are scheduled for low-occupancy windows, typically weekend nights or holiday periods. We confirm the access window in writing before scheduling and do not reschedule unilaterally.
