About Scottsdale as a Commercial Cleaning Market

  • Scottsdale's Financial Center district along Scottsdale Road between Thomas and Indian School houses a concentration of financial services and wealth management offices that are among the more demanding commercial cleaning clients in the Valley in terms of scope specificity and presentation standards.
  • The Old Town Scottsdale commercial district includes a mix of retail-adjacent office space and gallery buildings where floor care programs must account for polished concrete, specialty tile and occasional art-grade surfaces that require specific chemical protocols.
  • Scottsdale's North Scottsdale business corridor, particularly around the Loop 101 and Pima Road interchange, has a growing concentration of medical offices and outpatient facilities with restroom sanitation requirements that exceed standard commercial office programs.
