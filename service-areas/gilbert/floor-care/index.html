Floor Care for Gilbert Commercial Buildings

Hard-surface floor maintenance for Gilbert facilities is handled from our Tempe operations base, approximately 16 miles, 22–28 minutes from our Tempe operations base. Floor care programs in Gilbert are assessed and scheduled the same way as any other market: a walkthrough that identifies the floor type, the current condition and the access window available for scheduled program visits.

Example: A 2,400 sq ft Heritage District restaurant and bar space undergoing a daytime office conversion

The floor is original hardwood that has been refinished three times. The maintenance program covers monthly screening and buffing with a compatible hardwood finish product. No sanding or full refinishing is in scope—the floor has remaining life with a proper maintenance program, and sanding at this stage would reduce the board thickness below the threshold for future refinishing.

Floor Types We Service in Gilbert

  • VCT (vinyl composite tile) — strip, refinish, scrub-and-recoat
  • Polished concrete — low-abrasion burnishing, pH-appropriate maintenance
  • Sealed concrete — autoscrub, periodic resealing assessment
  • Commercial carpet tile — extraction, interim encapsulation, spot treatment
  • Specialty surfaces (travertine, terrazzo, hardwood) — assessed individually at walkthrough

Scheduling Around Gilbert Facilities

Floor care programs in Gilbert are scheduled for low-occupancy windows, typically weekend nights or holiday periods. We confirm the access window in writing before scheduling and do not reschedule unilaterally.
